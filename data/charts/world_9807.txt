KCMU plays a
broad selection of musical styles from around the world during its daily Variety Mix
programming. Three specialty shows focus on different aspects of world music. The Best Ambiance presents music from the African continent. Wo' Pop features an eclectic blend of world music. KCMU's ever
popular reggae program Positive Vibrations lays down a
hefty selection of roots reggae, dance hall, ska, rock steady, and dub.
