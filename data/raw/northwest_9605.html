The Northwest 10 consists of the latest releases from Northwest musicians of all persuasions and is included as a regular part of KCMU's daily Variety Mix programming. To hear shows that focus on music by local and Northwest artists, tune into Audioasis, the The Live Room, and Sonarchy.
