Chart Archive:

The variety charts list the most-played records on KEXP each week. The variety mix is a creative blend of alternative rock, hip hop, electronic, roots & blues, world & reggae, jazz, and more.

The variety mix is programmed during most of KEXP's 24-hour broadcasting day.
