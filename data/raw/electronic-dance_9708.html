RPM 10

The RPM 10 consists of the latest releases in techno, trip hop, acid jazz, ambient, dub, and related rhythmic grooves. The chart is reported weekly to the music trade magazine CMJ. The RPM 10 is included as a regular part of KCMU's daily Variety Mix programming. To get into a real groove, tune into Expansions and Sonarchy.
