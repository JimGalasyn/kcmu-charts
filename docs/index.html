The playlists survived.

KCMU published its own charts to the web from 1995, and the Wayback Machine kept them. 356 charts recovered — eight genres, 64 months, 7,847 entries, 2,410 distinct artists — plus year-end listener countdowns, a Top 90.3 of the decade, and 99 station reports printed in CMJ. Twenty-five artists from my Spotify playlist turn up in the real charts, several down to the exact track.

What was searched

Four leads, two of them live

KCMU broadcast from the University of Washington from 1972 until it became KEXP in 2001. Its logs turned out to be split across a defunct website, a trade magazine, and two boxes in a library basement.

kcmu.org, via the Wayback Machine 7,847 entries The station's own site, first captured November 1996 but carrying charts back to the week of 2 January 1995. 356 pages recovered across eight genre charts — Variety 60, Northwest 10, RPM 10 (electronic), Americana 40, Blues 10, World 10, Beat Box 10, Jazz 10 — plus year-end countdowns and a decade retrospective. This is the primary source and by far the cleanest.
CMJ New Music Report, via World Radio History 99 issues KCMU reported its charts weekly to CMJ, whose back issues are scanned as PDFs. 297 issues cover the KCMU era; 99 contain a KCMU station report, with the music director named. OCR on the two-column layout is rough — some blocks interleave with neighbouring stations.
UW Libraries Special Collections Offline only "KCMU records, 1971–1992" — 1.47 cubic feet, two boxes, explicitly including music play-lists and broadcasting schedules. Mostly open access; only the volunteer, internship, and personnel files are restricted. This is the sole route to the pre-web 1980s, and it requires a visit or a reproduction request.
College Radio Archive on SoundCloud Empty Indexed as a KCMU aircheck collection and cited in several histories, but the set and the entire uploader account contain zero tracks. Checked while signed in — not a permissions issue.

The RPM 10 · 1996–2000

The electronic chart, which is the one you want

KCMU described the RPM 10 as "the latest releases in techno, trip hop, acid jazz, ambient, dub, and related rhythmic grooves," folded into the daily Variety Mix and fed to the shows Expansions and Sonarchy. The chart was reported weekly to CMJ, which is why both archives carry it. Thirty-three monthly charts survive, March 1996 through February 2000 — the closest thing to a documented spine for your playlist.

The other seven charts

How wide the rotation actually was

The genre split is the real surprise. KCMU ran the Variety 60 as its main list and kept separate charts for Northwest, Americana, Blues, World, Beat Box and Jazz — so zydeco, Egyptian pop, Mississippi hill-country blues and drum & bass were all in rotation the same week. Any reconstruction built by genre adjacency alone, mine included, misses this completely.

Gaps

What is still missing

The 1980s. Nothing from the decade that shaped the taste in question is online in chart form. CMJ's own archive skips 1985–87 entirely and holds one issue from 1988. The UW boxes are the only known route, and they stop at 1992. This is where Psychic TV, SSQ, Danielle Dax, The Legendary Pink Dots and The Bongos would be documented, if anywhere.

1992 and 1993. World Radio History has no CMJ issues at all for those two years, and kcmu.org did not exist yet. The web charts begin the week of 2 January 1995.

Individual DJ shows. The recovered charts are station-level rotation, not what any one host played on a given night. Expansions and Sonarchy are named in the RPM chart blurbs, but their own logs were not published.

Airchecks. No recordings surfaced. The one indexed collection is empty.

Reproducing this

Where the harvest lives

Everything is on disk in the session scratchpad, along with the scripts that produced it. The Wayback CDX index must be filtered to statuscode:200 — the unfiltered index is full of 404 captures, which is what made the first pass fail.

kcmu_charts.py — scrapes kcmu.org chart pages from the Wayback Machine, caching raw HTML
parse_charts.py — parses all three chart layouts into kcmu_charts.csv (7,847 rows)
harvest.sh + extract_kcmu.py — pulls KCMU station reports out of 297 CMJ issue PDFs
overlap.py — cross-references the playlist against everything harvested
charts/ — 356 parsed charts · raw/ — cached HTML · kcmu_blocks/ — 99 CMJ reports

Three parsing traps worth recording: the title is italicised with <i> on the RPM pages but <em> on the Variety and Northwest pages; the 1999–2000 pages abandon italics entirely for an Artist - Title (Label) string; and a stray latin-1 byte in one page will kill a UTF-8 scrape mid-run.
