From its
humble beginnnings with its first broadcast in August 1993, The
Live Room has cast wide its net in gathering a wide spectrum of Northwest bands to
perform for KCMU listeners. Listed below by month of appearance are the bands that have
performed. To hear a remix of a performance
and to find more info on the bands, go to the highlighted band names below, and the The Live Room will provide you
with all the aural and bio goodies.
