Blues 10

Week of:

September 30-October 6, 1996

 The Blues 10 represents the best in new blues and R&B releases. The blues can be heard throughout the day as a part of KCMU's daily Variety Mix programming. To hear the blues and nothing but the blues, tune into Preachin' The Blues. For an added blues experience, KCMU blues DJ Marlee Walker will lead you there with Blues To Do.
